Reading the data

Values are the job-posting counts recorded by the collection process for each date and platform. They should not be interpreted as a count of unique new vacancies across the entire market. Platforms may differ in coverage and reporting; missing observations are shown as gaps, not zeroes.

Platforms A–D are anonymised Chinese recruitment sites. The index view uses the first date in your selected range with a positive observation for all four platforms as a common baseline. No index is shown when a common baseline is unavailable.

“Data through” comes from the observations themselves. Coverage may differ by platform; each statistic includes its own observation date. A recent page load does not imply fresh data.
